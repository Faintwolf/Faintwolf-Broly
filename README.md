![Rage](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Faintwolf/Faintwolf-Broly/main/readme/bar_rage.png)

A meter that fills over time and as you fight. When it's full, **hold F** to unleash it on top of any form:

- **Super armor**: no stuns, no knockback.
- More armor, damage, attack speed and regen.
- **50% bigger explosions** on every skill and ultimate.
- It drains while active, so keep hitting things to stay enraged. It never costs Ki.
- Builds faster and lasts longer in the Legendary forms, and longer the more max Ki he has.

![Broly roaring into Rage](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Faintwolf/Faintwolf-Broly/main/readme/rage.png)

![Controls](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Faintwolf/Faintwolf-Broly/main/readme/bar_controls.png)

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **F** (tap) | Transform into the next form |
| **F** (hold) | Unleash Rage |
| **X** (tap / hold) | Drop one form / go straight back to base |
| **V** | Your form's ultimate |
| **Jump** (hold) | Fly |

*F, X and V can be changed in the FaintAnimeUtilities settings.*

![Settings](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Faintwolf/Faintwolf-Broly/main/readme/bar_settings.png)

With **Risk Of Options** installed, Broly has his own page in the game's mod settings: cutscenes, the LSSJ floor, Ki drain, mastery requirements and rates, the HUD, and volume sliders for his voice, auras and attacks. The same settings are in the Broly and FaintAnimeUtilities config files (`Forms Need Mastery = false` under [Balance] gives every form from the start).
